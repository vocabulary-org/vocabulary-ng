Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Vocabulary uses cookies strictly for the operation and security of the Service. We categorise them as follows:

Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for the Service to function and do not require consent under GDPR (Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). They include:

  • Authentication cookies: set by Keycloak to manage your login session, verify your identity, and maintain your authenticated state.
  • Session cookies: used to maintain your session as you navigate the application.
  • Security cookies: used to support HTTPS enforcement and protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as language settings and display options, to provide a personalised experience. They are set based on actions you take (e.g. selecting a language).

3. Keycloak (Authentication Provider) Cookies

Our identity provider, Keycloak, sets cookies necessary for the authentication process. These are essential cookies required for login and session management. They are transmitted exclusively over HTTPS and are scoped to the authentication domain.

4. What We Do Not Use

We do not use:

  • Advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Third-party analytics cookies.
  • Social media cookies.

5. Data Transmitted via Cookies

All cookies are transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted connections only). Cookies used for authentication contain session identifiers and do not store your password or personal data directly. For details on how we process personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from logging in and using the Service. Common browser links for cookie management:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
