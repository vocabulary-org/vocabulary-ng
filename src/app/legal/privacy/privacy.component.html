Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vocabulary ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). This policy explains how we collect, process, and safeguard your information when you use our vocabulary learning application.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the processing of your personal data is the operator of Vocabulary. For questions regarding data protection, please contact us at vocabularyapporg@gmail.com.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): processing necessary to provide the vocabulary learning service you registered for.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): logging of IP addresses and user activity for security monitoring, fraud prevention, and operational stability.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we are required to retain data by applicable law.

4. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: name, email address, and authentication credentials managed through our identity provider (Keycloak).
  • Usage data: vocabulary lists, learning progress, language preferences, and interaction history within the application.
  • Log data: IP addresses, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and user activity logs. These are collected by both the frontend and backend for security monitoring and operational purposes.

5. How We Use Your Data

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining the vocabulary learning service.
  • Authenticating users and managing sessions via Keycloak.
  • Monitoring security threats, detecting unauthorised access, and preventing abuse.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and ensuring operational stability.
  • Improving the application and developing new features.

6. Data Sharing and Processors

We do not sell your personal data. Your data may be processed by the following categories of recipients:

  • Keycloak (identity provider): handles authentication and account management.
  • Hosting providers: infrastructure services that store and serve the application, subject to data processing agreements compliant with Article 28 GDPR.

7. International Transfers

If your data is transferred outside the EU/EEA or Switzerland, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an adequacy decision by the European Commission.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as follows:

  • Account and usage data: for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data is removed promptly.
  • Security and operational logs (IP addresses, activity): retained for a limited period necessary to detect and investigate security incidents, after which they are deleted.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15): obtain confirmation of whether your data is being processed and request a copy.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): request deletion of your data. You can delete your account directly in the application.
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18): request restriction of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at vocabularyapporg@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption enforced for all data in transit.
  • Secure authentication via Keycloak with industry-standard protocols.
  • Access controls and monitoring to detect unauthorised access.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us at vocabularyapporg@gmail.com.
