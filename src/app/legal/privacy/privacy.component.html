Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vocabulary ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). This policy explains how we collect, process, and safeguard your information when you use our vocabulary learning application.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the processing of your personal data is the operator of Vocabulary. For questions regarding data protection, please contact us at support@myvocabulary.net.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): processing necessary to provide the vocabulary learning service you registered for.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): where you have given explicit consent, such as accepting analytics cookies. You may withdraw your consent at any time via the cookie banner.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): logging of IP addresses and user activity for security monitoring, fraud prevention, and operational stability.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we are required to retain data by applicable law.

4. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Account data

Name, email address, and authentication credentials managed through our identity provider (Keycloak).

Usage data

Vocabulary lists, learning progress, language preferences, and interaction history within the application.

Log data

IP addresses, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and user activity logs collected for security and operations.

Analytics data

Anonymised usage data (pages visited, session duration, browser type) collected via Google Analytics only if you have given your consent.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing and maintaining the vocabulary learning service.
  • Authenticating users and managing sessions via Keycloak.
  • Monitoring security threats, detecting unauthorised access, and preventing abuse.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and ensuring operational stability.
  • Improving the application and developing new features.

6. Data Sharing and Processors

We do not sell your personal data. Your data may be processed by the following categories of recipients:

Processor Purpose
Keycloak (identity provider) Authentication and account management
Google LLC (Google Analytics) Anonymised usage analytics, only if you have given consent. Google may process data on servers outside the EEA. See Google's Privacy Policy.
Hosting providers Infrastructure services, subject to data processing agreements (Art. 28 GDPR)

7. International Transfers

If your data is transferred outside the EU/EEA or Switzerland, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an adequacy decision by the European Commission.

8. Data Retention

  • Account and usage data: retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data is removed promptly.
  • Security and operational logs: retained for a limited period necessary to detect and investigate security incidents, after which they are deleted.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

Access (Art. 15) — Obtain confirmation of whether your data is being processed and request a copy.

Rectification (Art. 16) — Request correction of inaccurate data.

Erasure (Art. 17) — Request deletion of your data. You can delete your account directly.

Restriction (Art. 18) — Request restriction of processing under certain conditions.

Portability (Art. 20) — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Object (Art. 21) — Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@myvocabulary.net. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Security

  • HTTPS encryption enforced for all data in transit.
  • Secure authentication via Keycloak with industry-standard protocols.
  • Access controls and monitoring to detect unauthorised access.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us at support@myvocabulary.net.
